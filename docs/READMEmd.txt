# Global Superstore Analytics — Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes **Global Superstore sales data (2011–2014)** to answer three critical business questions:
1. Overall profitability — Which categories and regions drive profit?
2. Margin optimization in Furniture — How do discounts impact profitability?
3. Customer & shipping efficiency — Which customers and shipping modes contribute most?

The solution is a **three‑page Power BI dashboard**:
- **Sales & Profitability Overview**
- **Furniture Margin Optimization**
- **Customer Profitability & Shipping Efficiency**

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Technology** → $664K profit, highest margin.
- **Furniture** → Losses occur when discounts exceed ~25% (Tables sub‑category).
- **Standard Class shipping** → $891K profit, most efficient mode.
- **Top customers** → Contribute 20%+ margins (e.g., Tamara Chand $8.7K).

##  How to Use
1. Download the PBIX file from "D:\Global-Superstore-Sales-Analysis\Powe BI\Sales_Analysis.pbix".
2. Open in **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use slicers (Year, Region, Segment, Category, Sub‑Category, Ship Mode, Discount Range).
4. Explore KPIs, trends, and insights interactively.

---

##  Business Recommendations
- Cap discounts at **25%** to prevent margin erosion.
- Promote **Standard Class shipping** for cost efficiency.
- Focus retention on **top 10 customers** driving high margins.
